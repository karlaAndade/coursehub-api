=== VERIFICACION EN POSTGRESQL: restricciones de students y enrollments ===
students | PRIMARY KEY | PRIMARY KEY (id)
students | UNIQUE | UNIQUE (email)
enrollments | FOREIGN KEY | FOREIGN KEY ("studentId") REFERENCES students(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
enrollments | FOREIGN KEY | FOREIGN KEY ("courseId") REFERENCES courses(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
enrollments | PRIMARY KEY | PRIMARY KEY (id)
enrollments | UNIQUE | UNIQUE ("studentId", "courseId")
